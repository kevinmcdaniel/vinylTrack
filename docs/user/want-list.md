# The want list

The want list is what to look for in a record store, and it tells you whether anyone in the family already has it. Open it with **Want list** at the top of any page.

## What you see

Wants are split into **Must-have** and **Nice-to-have**. Each one shows what's wanted (an album, or *Anything by* an artist), its notes, and a line saying who already has it:

- **Alex has one — Basement → Shelf 3**: someone has this record, and where it is. Your own copies say **You have one**.
- **Grandma Ruth has "Kind Of Blue" — Basement**: someone has a different pressing of the same record (same title and artist), or, for an *Anything by* want, an album by that artist.
- **No copies in Vinyl**: nobody has it in that collection.

The check only looks in the want's own collection, and only at collections that are yours or shared with you. If a collection isn't shared with you, its copies don't count, so "No copies" means "none that you can see".

With more than one collection, the buttons under the title show **All** or just one collection.

## Adding a want

Tap **Add a want**, or **Add to want list** on an album page to start with that album picked.

1. Pick the collection it belongs to.
2. Choose **An album** or **Anything by an artist**.
   - **An album**: pick one from the list, or type a new title and tick its artists. A new title is added to the collection with no copies, so it also shows when you browse.
   - **Anything by an artist**: pick the artist, or type a new name.
3. Choose **Must-have** or **Nice-to-have**, add a note if you like (*a mono pressing*), and tap **Add to want list**.

Pick from a list *or* type a new name, not both.

## Changing or removing a want

Tap **Edit** on a want to change its priority or notes. **Delete…** is inside Edit, so it takes a second tap.

## Found it

Tap **Found it** when you've bought one.

- The top of the page repeats who already has it. That's a warning, not a stop: sometimes a second copy is the point.
- Say where it's going and whose it is (you, unless you pick someone else), and add the condition, date and price if you like. You can add a new place or person right there.
- For an *Anything by* want, also say which album it is: one already in the collection, or a new title.

Tap **Save — it's found**. The want comes off the list and you land on the album page, which says where the new copy went. There's no undo, so check before you save.
