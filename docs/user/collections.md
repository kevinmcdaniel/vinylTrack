# Collections

A collection is one set of music kept together, like *Vinyl* or *Square Dance Calls*. Each one has its own albums, copies and storage places.

## Which ones you see

You only see collections that are yours or that someone shared with you. If one you expected is missing, ask the person it belongs to or the family admin.

## Switching

On a collection page, the box at the top names the collection you're in and how many albums it has. That counts albums, not copies: one album can have several copies. Pick another collection from the box to switch.

## Physical and digital

A collection is either **physical** (records, CDs, tapes) or **digital** (music files). Physical collections show each copy's condition and where and when it was bought. Digital ones leave that out.

## Starting a collection

Tap your name at the top right, then **New collection**. Give it a name, pick physical or digital, add notes if you like, and tap **Create collection**.

## Renaming and sharing

On a collection you own, tap **Settings**.

- **Rename** it, or change its notes, and tap **Save**. Whether it's physical or digital can't change.
- **Share with** someone: pick a family member who has been let in, and tap **Share**. They can see the collection and add to it: albums, copies, locations and wants. They can't rename it or share it on.
- To stop sharing, tap **Remove** under their name, then **Stop sharing**. It's gone from their list straight away.

A collection someone shared with you says whose it is under its name. Only its owner (or a family admin) sees **Settings**.

## Adding a record you have

On a collection, tap **Add copy**. First say which album it is:

- **Existing album** (the usual one): type part of the title and pick it from the list.
- **New album**: fill in the title, and the artists, format and year if you know them. If the collection already has an album that looks the same, you'll see it with **Use it instead**. You can still add a new one: a second pressing is fine.

Then say where it's going and whose it is (you, unless you pick someone else), add the condition, date and price if you like, and tap **Add copy**.

An album you don't have yet goes on the [want list](want-list.md) instead.
