# Getting started

## Signing in

Open vinylTrack and pick one:

- **Sign in with Google.** Use the Google account you gave the family admin.
- **Email me a sign-in link.** Type your email address and we send you a link. The link works once, for 15 minutes. If it doesn't arrive, check your spam folder, or ask for a new one.

Either way there is a quick "are you a person" check first. The buttons switch on once it's done.

You stay signed in on that device until you sign out, or until you go a month without using it.

## Waiting for approval

The first time you sign in, you'll see **Waiting for approval**. A family admin has to let you in before you can see any collections. You don't need to do anything; come back later.

If your request was declined, you'll see **Ask again**. That sends a new request to the admin.

## Signing out

Use **Sign out** at the top of the page.
