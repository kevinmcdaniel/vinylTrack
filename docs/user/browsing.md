# Finding things

## Search and filters

On a collection page:

- **Search by title**: type part of an album title and press Enter (Search or Go on a phone keyboard).
- **Any artist / Any format / Any genre**: pick one to narrow the list. They combine with the search and with each other.
- **Clear filters** puts the whole list back.

The page address keeps your search, so the back button works and you can send a filtered list to someone.

## An album

Tap an album to open it. You'll see its artists, format, year and genre, then every copy the family has:

- **where it is**, like *Basement → Shelf 3*
- **whose it is**. A copy lent out still shows its owner.
- for physical copies, its **condition** and where, when and for how much it was bought

"Nobody owns a copy of this yet" means it's listed but nobody has one.

**Add copy** records one you have. **Add to want list** puts the album on the [want list](want-list.md).

## An artist

Tap an artist's name to see everything of theirs you can see, across your collections.
